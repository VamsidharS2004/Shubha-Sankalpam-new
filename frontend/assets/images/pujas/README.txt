PUJA CARD & DETAILS PHOTOS
============================
Photos for individual puja cards and their details-page carousel.

Recommended: at least one photo per puja, square-ish or 4:3, named
to match the puja, e.g.:
  go-grasam-sankalpam.jpg
  lakshmi-kubera-homam.jpg

To use these, open content/pujas.js and add an "image" field to that
puja's entry, e.g.  image: "assets/images/pujas/go-grasam-sankalpam.jpg"
— then update assets/js/cards.js and assets/js/pages/details.js to use
that field instead of the colored placeholder (ask for help if needed).
