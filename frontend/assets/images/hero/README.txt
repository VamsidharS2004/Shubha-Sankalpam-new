HERO IMAGES
============
Photos for the homepage hero slider (the big banner images).

Recommended: 1 photo per puja shown in the hero, landscape orientation,
at least 1200×900px, named after the puja, e.g.:
  go-grasam-sankalpam.jpg
  shravana-somavara-shiva.jpg

To use a photo here, open home.html, find the hero slide's placeholder
<svg>...<span class="label">Replace with your puja photo</span> block
(search for "REPLACE WITH REAL PHOTO" in engine/pages logic — see
assets/js/pages/home.js), and swap it for:
  <img src="assets/images/hero/your-photo.jpg" alt="Puja name">
