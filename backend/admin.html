How to see the exact ad and campaign here

Other platforms (YouTube / Google Ads, X ...): add ?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=YOUR-CAMPAIGN to the ad's destination link (use utm_source=x for X). The name you type in utm_source is what shows in the table. Google, X, TikTok and Bing clicks are counted as ad clicks even without it.

Meta (Facebook / Instagram):

In Meta Ads Manager open your ad, go to Tracking → URL parameters and paste this line. Meta fills in the placement (Facebook / Instagram), the campaign name and the ad name by itself:

utm_source={{site_source_name}}&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}

Without it, clicks from Meta ads are still counted (Meta adds its own click id), but they may show as “Meta ads” without the campaign name.

Booking Form

How to edit the booking form
  1. Change the wording in Core labels. Blank translations use the standard wording.
  2. Use Optional fields to show/hide gotram or sankalpam, or mark them required.
  3. Click Add Field for a new question; choose its type and enter its label.
  4. Check the preview below and click Save Form. Changes apply to future bookings.

Edit wording and optional fields, or add up to 12 extra fields. WhatsApp, devotee/karta name and the special puja gotram stay protected. Prices, payment controls and login cannot be changed here. Existing booking answers keep their original field labels.
